Our Approach

“We don’t override the body.
We listen to it.”

Founded in 1997 by naturopath George Weinand, Neurowaves grew out of a simple conviction: the body already carries the ability to heal itself, and most of the work is removing what’s in its way. Nearly three decades later, that same conviction still runs the practice: non-invasive, whole-person, and unhurried.

Today it’s carried on by Jeff and Dolly Erickson, alongside Gayle Weinand, seeing patients out of an actual house in Dana Point, where the waiting room really is a living room.
