Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 28, 2026

Overview

Manscaped Outdoors ("Manscaped Outdoors," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you visit our website or submit a request through our contact and estimate forms, how we use that information, and the choices you have. By using this site, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Information We Collect

When you submit a contact or estimate request through our website, we may collect:

  • Your name, phone number, and email address
  • Your project location or nearest community
  • Project type, approximate budget, and desired timeline
  • A description of your project
  • Photos or videos you choose to upload

We may also automatically collect certain information when you visit our site, including:

  • IP address, browser type, and device type
  • Referring website, the page that led you to us, and pages you viewed on our site
  • Approximate location based on standard web analytics
  • Information passed along by ad platforms when you arrive from an ad, such as the campaign or ad that referred you

Cookies, Pixels, and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies, including the Meta (Facebook and Instagram) Pixel, to understand how visitors use our site and to measure and improve the performance of our advertising. These tools may collect information about your visit and actions on our site and share it with Meta Platforms, Inc. and other advertising partners so we can show you more relevant ads and measure results. We may also use similar tools from other advertising and analytics providers, such as Google.

You can control cookies through your browser settings, and you can manage how Meta uses your data for ads through your Meta Ad Preferences. Turning off cookies may limit some features of our site.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your inquiry and provide a quote or estimate
  • Communicate with you about your project by phone, text, or email
  • Improve our website, services, and marketing
  • Measure and improve the performance of our online advertising
  • Maintain records of our customer and lead relationships

How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with service providers who help us run our business and marketing, including our customer relationship management (CRM) platform, our website and hosting providers, and advertising and analytics partners such as Meta and Google as described above. These providers are only permitted to use your information to provide services to us.

Data Retention

We keep the information you submit for as long as needed to respond to your inquiry, maintain our business records, and comply with legal or accounting requirements.

Your Choices and Rights

You can:

  • Ask us to update or correct your information
  • Ask us to delete your information from our records
  • Opt out of marketing calls, texts, or emails at any time by replying "stop" or contacting us directly
  • Adjust your ad preferences directly with Meta or Google

To make a request, contact us using the information below.

Children's Privacy

Our website and services are intended for adults seeking outdoor design-build services and are not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Data Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect the information we collect. No method of transmission or storage over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Third-Party Links

Our site may link to third-party sites, such as Facebook. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites and encourage you to review their policies.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

Contact Us
